# Namma Dina art direction

## Intent

Namma Dina should feel like a quiet observatory beside a well-used notebook: calm, personal, and precise. The Aurora identity belongs in the environment and in small moments of progress. It should never compete with a task, a check-in, or a person’s name.

The dark theme is a deep green-black sky with layered forest surfaces. Emerald and teal identify actions and progress; mint carries emphasis; soft amber marks sunrise or a gentle nudge. The light theme, **Aurora Day**, uses warm paper-like surfaces and the same green family. Neither theme relies on a large decorative gradient to create hierarchy.

## Reference study

These products informed design principles only. No layout, copy, illustration, or brand asset is being copied.

- [Headspace](https://www.headspace.com/) makes a broad wellbeing product feel welcoming through warm language and clear content groupings.
- [Sunsama](https://www.sunsama.com/) foregrounds a manageable daily plan and gives planning a deliberate beginning and end.
- [Todoist](https://www.todoist.com/) keeps task capture direct and makes “today” easy to scan.
- [Linear Mobile](https://linear.app/mobile) shows how a dense product can keep its mobile actions focused and compact.
- [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/app) keeps its dark workspace quiet and uses a concentrated ambient glow to frame the main interaction.
- [Google Flow](https://flow.google.com/about) uses cinematic imagery against a dark canvas; Namma Dina borrows the depth and careful contrast while keeping real tasks readable.
- [GPT-6 Astra](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/) uses a deep star field and restrained points of light to suggest scale without filling the view with decoration.

The user-provided Borealis reference adds layered aurora ribbons, fine vertical rays, and a natural night sky. Namma Dina combines those cues with its bilingual voice, Aurora palette, gentler pace, and Karnataka-aware but non-stereotyped details.

## Visual language

- **Surfaces:** midnight base, raised forest surfaces, and a brighter overlay for menus and dialogs. Separate them with crisp one-pixel borders and controlled shadows; reserve blur for the fixed navigation chrome.
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans for headings, DM Sans for Latin interface text, and Noto Sans Kannada for Kannada. Use tabular numerals for dates, quantities, and progress. Body copy stays at 15–16 px or larger; utility labels are at least 12 px.
- **Iconography:** Lucide outline icons at a consistent 1.8 px stroke, with 44 px interaction targets. The Namma Dina mark is a custom sunrise/horizon motif. Use original inline SVGs for empty states, not emoji or stock art.
- **Composition:** editorial alignment, compact date controls, and a clear lead item. The My Day screen answers what matters now, what comes next, and how the day is going. Cards have distinct roles and weights rather than repeating one glass panel.
- **Motion:** restrained and brief. The procedural Aurora sky stays behind the shell, with layered emerald, teal, and faint violet ribbons, a fine star field, a low-contrast horizon, and a dark vignette. Motion communicates state; it does not decorate reading surfaces. Keep the rays soft behind cards, pause when the page is hidden, and use a still frame for reduced-motion and calm settings.
- **Voice:** kind, specific, and free of pressure. Kannada copy should read naturally, not as a word-for-word translation.

## Never do

- No stock purple/blue gradient hero, heavy glow, or glass blur behind long text.
- No identical rounded-card grid, centered-everything dashboard, or decorative gradient with no job.
- No tiny uppercase labels, low-contrast muted text, emoji icons, vague slogans, lorem ipsum, or filler copy.
- No cultural shorthand or stereotypes in Karnataka-inspired details.
- No assistant or animation that interrupts a form, shames a user, or changes a schedule without approval.

## Milestone 1 scope

Define the Aurora and Aurora Day tokens, typography, reusable components and states; update the shared app shell; redesign My Day; and establish the procedural sky direction. Later milestones handle the Mitra 3D asset decision and remaining screens. Milestone 1 ends with a screenshot and recording review before those later stages begin.
