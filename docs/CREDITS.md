## Original product artwork

The sunrise/horizon mark and the inline empty-state illustration are original Namma Dina SVG/CSS artwork. The existing Lucide outline icons are from the `lucide-react` package and are licensed under ISC; they are used as interface icons, not as a substitute for custom brand art.

The procedural Northern Lights field is an original Canvas 2D implementation. The user-provided Borealis page was used as a visual reference; its shader, video, and other source assets were not copied into the app.

## Reference products

Headspace, Sunsama, Todoist, Linear Mobile, Gemini, Google Flow, and GPT-6 Astra are cited in `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md` as product-design references only. No brand assets or layouts were reused.
